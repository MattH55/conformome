# Cover letter (draft for the author to edit and sign)

To the Editorial Team, Open Research Europe

Dear Editors,

I am submitting *Conformome: an index of experimental evidence on the conformational states of proteins and nucleic acids, mapped across techniques* for consideration as a **Software Tool Article**.

Conformome (https://conformome.org) collects experimental and computational entries on biomolecular conformation from ten public sources (the RCSB PDB, BMRB, SASBDB, DisProt, AlphaFold DB, MDDB, SMASH-BIO, NAKB, Rfam and RMDB), records each with its technique, construct, conditions, value with uncertainty and provenance, and maps the entries onto curated conformational states so that the support for each state can be read across techniques. Where comparable measurements on the same state disagree, the disagreement is flagged rather than averaged. The pilot release covers 81 proteins and six nucleic acids. The pipeline is open source (MIT) and every number on the site and in the article is regenerated from the raw inputs recorded in a manifest.

The work is eligible for Open Research Europe through the Horizon 2020 Marie Skłodowska-Curie Innovative Training Network AntiHelix (grant agreement No 859853), of which it continues the conformational-ensemble and FAIR-data work; it ingests SMASH-BIO, an output of the same project.
